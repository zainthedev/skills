# Resources stay free, and a tutor is optional

dojo assigns free resources only. Many good language resources are paid, and many are free: public broadcasters' learner services, YouTube channels with graded input, free podcasts, national institutes' curricula, dictionaries and frequency lists. lingo keeps dojo's rule: assigned resources are free, a free tier counts when the part assigned is free, and paid or region-locked resources go under Excluded. Lesson zero and the task rules recommend a person to talk to, a tutor or a free language exchange, as optional practice, and never require a paid service.

## Consequences

- Some languages have thin free input at some levels; the ledger's thin-evidence note says so.
