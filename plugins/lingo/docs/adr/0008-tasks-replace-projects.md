# Tasks replace projects

A dojo project is a build with requirements. The language equivalent is a communicative task: something people do with a language, with an outcome another person could check, such as a voice message, an email or an order at a counter. Task-based teaching has meta-analytic support with method caveats (Bryfonski and McKay 2019; evidence section 3). A guided task, a model text with gaps, comes first at A0 and A1, as dojo's completion project does for beginners. The capstone is derived from the goal, and when the goal names an exam, it is a timed mock from a free official sample.

## Consequences

- Tasks name the skills they exercise, and the syllabus spreads them in the profile's Focus order.
- A speaking task asks for a recording the learner keeps and compares with native input; lingo cannot hear it, and says so.
